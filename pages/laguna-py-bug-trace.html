py-bug-trace — Laguna model sweep

Evaluation of poolside/laguna-xs.2 against 28 comparison models on the py-bug-trace environment from the Poolside Laguna hackathon. Models predict outputs of subtly broken Python (L1–L2) or fix buggy code (L3). All artifacts originate from the laguna-eval-experiments dataset on Hugging Face.

target: poolside/laguna-xs.2 29 models matrix: 2026-06-04 reports: 2026-07-17
Headline finding: Laguna-XS.2 ranks 12th of 29 overall (79.0%) but 2nd on Level 3 (86.7%) — an inverted difficulty profile where the target is weak on easy tasks and strong on hard code-fix problems. Thirteen of 28 flagged anomalies are level inversions across the field.
